# Outposts and Guards

Each of the seven factions has a small outpost with a Faction Registry, Contract
Board, bound Trade Terminal, faction banner, Envoy and two Guards. The nearest
outpost can be located with `/locate structure nerofactions:outpost/<faction>`
where the player has permission to use that command.

Speak to the Envoy to open the local terminal's offers. The terminal and Envoy
refuse visitors whose standing is below the server's hostility threshold or whose
allegiance and Trusted standing place them with a rival. Rival Members below Trusted
pay a surcharge.

Guards defend their Envoy and patrol only within the outpost. Guards and Envoys
are dismounted from boats, minecarts and other vehicles, so they cannot be carried
away from their outpost. Space Guild, Nero
Corporation and Salvagers Guards shoot arrows. The other factions fight in melee.
Killing a Guard costs Combat standing with its faction and awards capped Combat
standing with its enemies.

A discovery records the faction and dimension for your export and paired
companion app. See [Configuration](Configuration) for generation and recording
switches and [Privacy](Privacy) for export and erasure.

**On maps.** With a map mod that reads Neroland Core's map registry, an outpost shows on your map only while you are within 48 blocks of it, with your standing (hostile, neutral or friendly); the map never points you at outposts you have not reached.
