# Faction gear

Signature tools have Member-tier recipes. Faction armour has Trusted-tier
recipes. The special effects require active membership, and armour bonuses
also require all four matching pieces. The tooltip shows the equipped count
and which requirement is missing. Each piece keeps its base protection when
the full-set bonus is inactive.

| Set | Bonus | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Union Exosuit | Haste I and reduced fall and explosion damage underground. | Slowness I in exposed daylight. |
| Guild Flight Suit | Fall protection, Slow Falling when sneaking in the air and 10% faster movement. | Two fewer points of armour toughness and 15% more melee damage. |
| Void Vestments | Melee life steal and sight of invisible entities. | 20% more damage in exposed daylight and a greater detection radius for rival Guards. |
| Corporate Power Suit | Two extra attack damage and Resistance I at full health. | Hunger exhaustion is 50% higher. |
| Authority Environment Suit | Water breathing; Poison is cleared and deals no damage, and freezing deals no damage. | One less attack damage. |
| Colonist Field Gear | Regeneration I while standing still and two extra hunger points from food. | Lowest base armour of the seven sets. |
| Salvager Patchwork | A one-in-four chance to preserve tool durability. The first time you open a loot container that has not been opened yet, its own loot table is rolled once more and the extra items go into empty slots only. | Patchwork armour itself wears 25% faster on average. |

## Settler's Multitool

The Multitool mines with an axe, hoe or shovel head depending on the block. Its axe
and hoe right-click actions (stripping logs, tilling soil; sneak forces the hoe) need
Free Colonists membership at Member standing. Anyone else gets shovel behaviour on use.
The automatic mining-speed switch is not membership-gated.

## Trims and patterns

At Inner Circle, each faction awards a reusable loom pattern and enough
matching smithing templates and inlays to trim a full set.
